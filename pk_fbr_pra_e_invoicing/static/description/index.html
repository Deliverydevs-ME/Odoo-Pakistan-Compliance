FBR & PRA Digital Invoicing for Odoo

Pakistan Compliance

Submit Customer Invoices and Point of Sale invoices to Pakistan's FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) digital invoicing API and the PRA (Punjab Revenue Authority) IMS, directly from Odoo — with per-company configuration, full pre-submission validation, and a reliable automatic retry queue.

Government E-Invoicing, Handled Correctly the First Time

Pakistan's digital invoicing mandates require every qualifying Customer Invoice to be reported to FBR and, for Punjab-registered sales, to PRA as well — in a specific JSON format, with specific field validation, and with specific rules about what a government system will and will not accept. A single malformed submission can mean a rejected invoice, a blocked delivery, or a compliance gap that surfaces months later during an audit.

This module connects your existing Odoo Sales, Accounting, and Point of Sale data to both authorities automatically. It checks every required field before an invoice is even allowed to post, builds the exact payload each authority expects, submits it, and reads back the real result — not just an HTTP status code, but the authority's own acceptance or rejection message.

The result is a Customer Invoice workflow your sales and accounting teams already know, with Pakistan e-invoicing compliance running underneath it instead of alongside it.

How an Invoice Gets to FBR and PRA

From company setup to a government-issued invoice number.

STEP 01

Configure Each Company's FBR / PRA Settings

On every Pakistani company's FBR and ePRA tabs, set Sandbox Testing or Production mode, the API endpoint and authorization token (FBR), the PRA POS ID (PRA), and the company's own province. Each company is configured independently.

Saving these settings — or simply enabling FBR/PRA for the first time — automatically runs the Pakistan Chart of Accounts setup described above for that company.

STEP 02

Tag Products and Customers Once

Each product gets an HS Code, an FBR Sale Type (28 official FBR scenarios ship pre-loaded), and optionally a PRA PCT Code — synced on demand from FBR's own live reference API, over 7,800 codes, with a single click. Each customer gets a province and, when registered, an NTN/STN or CNIC.

Tag it once on the product or customer record and every future invoice line inherits it automatically — no repeated data entry per invoice.
STEP 03

Post the Invoice, Then Submit

Confirming a Customer Invoice runs the full FBR/PRA validation and, when either authority applies, forces the posting date to today (a genuine FBR/PRA same-day-filing requirement, not an Odoo default). Post to FBR and Post to PRA buttons appear on posted, eligible invoices — or submit many at once from the Customer Invoices list with the bulk Submit to FBR / Submit to PRA actions.

Point of Sale invoices are the one exception: they submit to FBR automatically the moment they post, with no button to click.

STEP 04

A Queue That Doesn't Overwhelm the Government API

Every submission goes through a shared FBR/PRA queue that processes one request at a time, in order — deliberately, since both authorities' APIs are rate-sensitive government systems, not something to hit with concurrent requests. Network errors and transient failures retry automatically (up to five attempts); a scheduled job sweeps every 15 minutes to recover anything stuck or due for retry.

Business rejections — the authority telling you the data itself is wrong — are never silently retried; they surface immediately so you can fix the actual problem.

STEP 05

Every Attempt Is Logged, Nothing Is a Black Box

Every submission — successful or not — is recorded with the exact request payload, the exact response the authority sent back, and a plain-language result posted straight to the invoice's own chatter. Once FBR or PRA accepts an invoice, its own compliant print report becomes available, carrying the government-issued invoice number and a QR code.

WHAT COUNTS AS SUCCESS
A 200 OK from the API is not acceptance. Only the authority's own "Valid" / "Code 100" response is.

Before You Switch to Production

This module ports the logic of the widely-used ERPNext "Pak Compliance" app to Odoo 18, adapted to Odoo's own accounting and tax engine rather than translated line by line. It depends only on Odoo's standard account, l10n_pk, and point_of_sale apps.

HS Codes, PRA PCT Codes, and FBR Sale Type scenarios assigned to your real products should be verified against the actual Pakistan Customs Tariff and your own tax treatment by whoever handles your company's tax compliance — this module gets the structure, validation, and submission mechanics right; the correct classification for your specific goods and services is a business decision, not something software should guess silently.

Test thoroughly in Sandbox Testing mode against real product and customer data before switching any company to Production.
